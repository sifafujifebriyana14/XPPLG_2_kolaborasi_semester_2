My Experience Designing a Website

Recount Text

My Experience Designing a Website in Figma Sifa, a student majoring in RPL, had a very memorable experience designing a website using Figma for my second collaborative assignment. The assignment challenged me to create an attractive and professional-looking portfolio design.

At first, I felt quite confused and a little hesitant because I wasn't used to creating designs from scratch in Figma. However, I didn't give up. I began looking for inspiration from various designs online and trying to understand how to create a neat and pleasing layout. After that, I began arranging important sections like the navbar, hero section, and portfolio in a style that I liked.

During the design process, I tried to choose colors that suited my personality and tried various font combinations for a more aesthetic look. Although I struggled and had to make several design changes, I persisted until I achieved the best result.

Finally, I successfully completed the website design myself and felt proud. When I showed it to my teacher for a consultation, she approved my design. This experience not only improved my design skills but also taught me the importance of patience, creativity, and a willingness to continue learning.
