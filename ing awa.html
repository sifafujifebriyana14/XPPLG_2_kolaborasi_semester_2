LEAF WASTE

Waste is anything that is the remainder or waste production process, (both industrial and domestic). And waste no longer has any value or benefit, and can be causes negative impacts on the environment and health if not managed properly. Waste can be categorized based on its form (solid, liquid. gas), its costituent compounds (organik, inorganic, B3), and its source (domestic, Industrial, agricultura). Proper waste management, such as recycling, composting, and waste water treatment plants (WWTPS). is important to reduce its impact on health and the environment.

Based on my observations, leaf waste is a natural organic waste derived from plant remains, which is easily biodegradable. Althought often considered a problem, leaf Waste has great potential as a renewable resource that can be processed into compost, paper raw materials, alternative fuels, handicrafts, or mixed with other construction materials.

Based on its source, leaf waste is included in natural waste because leaf waste it self is waste produced from the plant cycle, which will eventually shed its leaves. Leaf waste can also be generated from agricultural waste originating from agricultural or plantation activities. The characteristics of leaves include the presence of petioles, stalks and leaf blades, as well as the green color due to chlorophyll which plays a role in photos thesis. The types of leaves are differentiated based on the arrangement of the leaf veins and the shape of the leaf blade, namely pinnate (like fish fins), palmate (like fingers), curved (curved from base to tip), and parallel (obes). Other main characteristics of leaves incluede green color due to chlorophyll, flat structure, and the presence of a midrib, stalk, and leaf blade. Based on leaf structure, there are simple leaves, compound leaves, and needle leaves.

Leaf waste has the benefit of being composted as an organic fertilizer that can fertilize the soil and reduce the amount of waste that pollutes the environment. This leaf can also be used as a mixture of industrial materials such as cement board, basic fuel for handicraft products that have economic value, and as a medium for ecoprinting. Leaf waste can be used as a material for briquette briquettes, an environmentally friendly alternative form of Fuel.
