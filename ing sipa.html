LEAF WASTE

Leaf waste is residual material or waste generated from a production process or activity, both from industry and households (domestic). It has the potential to cause negative impacts on the environment and human health. In this observation, I will recycle leaf waste. I will turn this leaf waste into ecoprint on a mug (an ecoprint pattern on a mug).

Leaf waste used for ecoprinting can come from many types of leaves, such as teak, papaya, guava, mango, cherry, hibiscus, and starfruit leaves. However, teak leaves are most commonly used for ecoprinting because they produce a reddish-brown color that is beautiful and shows clear fiber patterns. Therefore, in this observation, I will use teak leaves.

1 Leaf waste for ecoprint refers to plant residues like leaves that can be used as a natural dye source on cloth. This helps reduce organic waste at home. Using leaf waste like teak leaves aims to create natural motifs. Thus, the leaf waste becomes useful waste.

Leaf waste in ecoprint has benefits as it can be turned into compost, which serves as organic fertilizer that can enrich the soil and reduce the amount of waste that pollutes the environment. The processing of leaf waste in ecoprint can also improve soil quality and support a more sustainable lifestyle. This textile dyeing technique is environmentally friendly by using pigments derived from plants.
