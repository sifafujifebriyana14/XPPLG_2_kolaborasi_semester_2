LEAF WASTE

Waste is residual or discarded material generated from production processes or activities from both industry and households, the presence of which has the potential to cause negative impacts on the environment and living things. Such as the emergence of various diseases and other things. In this observation, I will describe leaf waste.

Leaf waste is the residual parts of plants that have fallen either naturally or artificially. Such as illegal logging, pruning branches, and picking leaves for animal feed. This waste will usually become trash and a breeding ground for bacteria if not managed properly. Conversely, if it can be utilized well, it will produce useful products for life.

Leaf waste is usually found under trees. These leaves are usually brown with very unique and different patterns of color. The type of leaf can be distinguished by its different size and shape, and has a different thickness and length depending on the type.

Leaf waste is very beneficial if managed very well and correctly. If not managed properly or is just burned, it will cause various new problems such as pollution and disease. Leaves are very useful and can be made into eco-prints on ceramic cups, fertilizer for plants, and so on. If leaves can be utilized correctly by the right person, they will get a profit, and vice versa if they are not managed by the right person, they will get new problems.
